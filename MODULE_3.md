# BrainBrick Module 3 — Products

Module 3 builds the first complete product catalogue and product-detail architecture.

## Included

- Product catalogue page
- Product category filtering
- Six initial product concepts/placeholders
- Reusable ProductCard component
- Product data separated from UI
- Individual product-detail route: `/products/:slug`
- Product visual placeholders for future real photographs
- Product-stage/status labels
- WhatsApp and contact CTAs
- Responsive desktop/tablet/mobile layout

## Important

The product names, descriptions, features and statuses in this module are intentionally presented as concepts/placeholders. They are not claims of final specifications, pricing, availability, certification or regulatory status.

## Example routes

- `/products`
- `/products/starter-line-follower`
- `/products/brainbrick-controller`
- `/products/smart-sensor-bot`
- `/products/robotics-mechanics-kit`
